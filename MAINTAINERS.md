# Maintainers

This document lists the current maintainers of the KOD project, their
areas of ownership, and the process for becoming a maintainer.

## Current maintainers

| Name | Handle | Areas |
|------|--------|-------|
| KOD Team lead | [@elcoosp](https://github.com/elcoosp) | All |

## Responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to:

- Review and merge pull requests.
- Triage issues and respond to security reports per [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Cut releases following the process in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Maintain the code quality baseline documented in `docs/HANDOFF-*.md`.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who have demonstrated sustained, high-quality contributions
(features, reviews, documentation, or issue triage) for a period of several
months may be invited to become maintainers. The existing maintainers make
the decision by consensus.

Nomination, review, and voting follow the project's convention of discussion
in a GitHub issue titled `Maintainer nomination: <handle>`.

## Emeritus maintainers

Maintainers who step down after a period of active contribution are recorded
here (currently none).
